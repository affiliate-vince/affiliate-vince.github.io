USX MOUNT

A TV mount you bolt to the wall once and forget — ours has held a 55" OLED for three years without a millimeter of sag.

USX MOUNT comes from a factory that has designed and built TV wall mounts for more than 20 years. Its flagship XMM006 full-motion mount is UL certified, fits most 26-65 inch TVs up to 75 lbs, extends 16.7 inches from the wall for corner and angled seating, and retracts to 2.8 inches when you want it flat. At the time of writing it sits at 4.7 stars from more than 51,000 Amazon ratings, with 6,000+ bought in the past month.

why i recommend

three reasons this brand stands out

UL certification, not just marketing

The mount is UL listed — independently tested for load and safety — and the bracket is made from heavy-gauge steel on automated production lines. Reviewers routinely hang 55-65 inch TVs on it and stop thinking about it.

Real range of motion

Swivels up to 90° left and right, tilts +5°/-15°, and has +/-5° of post-install leveling. That's enough to pull a TV out of a corner or point it at a bed without moving the wall plate.

Install kit that respects your time

Hardware arrives sorted in labeled bags with a bubble level and a paper template included. Several reviews describe hanging the TV in 15 minutes — the most common complaint in 1-star reviews is measuring twice, which no mount can fix.

who it's for

honest match — no fluff

perfect for
  • Anyone mounting a 26-65 inch TV on wood studs or solid concrete
  • Rooms where the seating is off-center and the TV needs to swing toward it
  • Buyers who want a UL-listed bracket and a complete hardware kit, not a trip to the hardware store
skip it if
  • Your TV is over 65 inches or heavier than 75 lbs — USX makes longer-arm and bigger-capacity models for that
  • You only have drywall with no stud or block behind it — this mount needs a real anchor point
  • You want a flush, non-moving mount — that is a different product (and they make those too)
