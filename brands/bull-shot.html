Bull Shot

The carpet spot remover in a shaker can — 4.0 stars from 7,200+ buyers who actually spilled things.

Bull Shot is an American-made line of carpet and upholstery cleaners sold in aerosol cans — no machines, no mixing, no rinsing. The Pro formula foams on contact and lifts coffee, wine, grease, tracked-in dirt and pet accidents out of carpet, rugs and fabric furniture. The brand has been a steady Amazon performer: the Pro can alone carries 7,200+ ratings, and the wider lineup (Max Strength, Pet Pro enzyme, degreaser) is pitched on TV spots as the point-and-spray answer to stains.

why i recommend

three reasons this brand stands out

Professional-strength foam, no machine

More surfactants than the original formula means the foam clings to carpet fibers and works the stain from the inside. You shake, point, spray, and walk away — no rental carpet machine.

Built for pet accidents too

The Pet Pro variant adds enzyme action aimed at urine and odor, and the base formula handles the usual suspects: coffee, wine, soda, blood, lipstick, tracked dirt.

Water-based and American-made

Water-based formula, no sticky residue, and made in the USA. Reviewers with kids and pets call it the first stain cleaner that does not require gloves and ventilation.

who it's for

honest match — no fluff

perfect for
  • Carpet and rug owners with kids or pets
  • Fabric sofas and car interiors
  • Old set-in stains — foam sits on the fiber instead of running off
skip it if
  • Whole-room deep cleaning (that is a machine job)
  • Non-colorfast or delicate fabrics — spot-test first
  • You expect a single spray to erase a stain that has been there for years
