Veradek

Frost-proof planters that look like stone and weigh like plastic — 4.8 stars from 6,600+ gardeners.

Veradek makes modern outdoor planters and patio furniture from a polypropylene composite that fakes heavy materials without the weight or the cracking. The Pure Series tapered planter is their flagship: 30 inches tall, frost-proof, UV-treated against fading, with a removable inner shelf so you can plant directly or drop in a liner. 6,600+ ratings at 4.8 stars. The catalog extends to Block planters, side tables, umbrella tables and more — all aimed at the same audience: people who want modern outdoor styling that survives winters.

why i recommend

three reasons this brand stands out

Survives the freeze-thaw cycle

Plastic composite is frost-proof where ceramic and concrete crack — the #1 reason cold-climate gardeners end up with a driveway full of Veradek.

Stone looks, movable weight

A 30-inch tapered planter you can actually drag across a patio when the sun moves — try that with the concrete version.

Color that does not quit

UV-treated finish resists the fading that turns cheap planters chalky after one summer, and the sharp modern silhouette reads expensive from across the yard.
