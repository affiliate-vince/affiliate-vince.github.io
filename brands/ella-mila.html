ella+mila

Nail strengthener with a cult following — 10,000+ ratings and a name that keeps showing up in r/nails.

ella+mila is a vegan, PETA-certified cruelty-free nail care brand whose First Aid Kiss strengthener/hardener has become the default answer for weak, peeling nails. It is a clear formula you wear over bare nails or as a base — reviewers report visibly harder, faster-growing nails within weeks — and it doubles as a protective top layer. 10,000+ ratings at 4.4 stars, with a supporting line of quick-dry top coats, growth oil and (in their wider catalog) 7-free polishes.
