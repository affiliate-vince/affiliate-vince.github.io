SACHEU

The peel-off lip stain from your TikTok feed — 22,000+ reviews later, still polarizing and still selling.

SACHEU STAY-N is the peel-off lip liner/stain that blew up on TikTok: you draw it on like a lip liner, let it set 5-7 minutes, peel off the film, and what is left is a smudge-proof tint that stays through eating and drinking. The formula adds hyaluronic acid and vitamin E, and it is vegan, fragrance-free and cruelty-free. 22,000+ ratings at a very honest 4.0 — it works exactly as shown for some, and it is fiddly or patchy for others, which is the real review story.

why i recommend

three reasons this brand stands out

Tint that survives lunch

The peel-off film leaves a stain that does not kiss off, drink off or eat off — the entire appeal, and why it went viral as the "all-day lip" trick.

Actually skin-friendly

Hyaluronic acid and vitamin E in the formula, and the line is vegan, fragrance-free and cruelty-free — cleaner than most drugstore long-wear lip products.

One tool, many looks

Precision-tip applicator lets you overline, ombre or full-fill; the shade range is built around the p-INKED signature red plus neutrals.

who it's for

honest match — no fluff

perfect for
  • People who love trying viral beauty hacks
  • Lipstick-wearers tired of reapplying after meals
  • Minimal-makeup days when you want color that stays
skip it if
  • You hate fussy multi-step makeup — this takes practice
  • You have chapped or peeling lips (apply on clean, dry lips only)
  • You expect an opaque lipstick finish — it is a stain, not a cream
