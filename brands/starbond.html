Starbond

The woodworker's CA glue that has been made in small batches since 1988.

Starbond has been making cyanoacrylate (CA) glue since 1988, in small batches so it ships fresh. Their thin 'Super Fast' formula is the go-to for woodturners and woodworkers: it sets in 2-3 seconds, wicks into cracks and micro-fractures, and works on wood, metal, rubber, ceramics and 3D prints. The Pro kit includes precision micro-tips and an anti-clog cap — the accessories that make thin CA actually usable. 14,900+ ratings at 4.6 stars, with 3,000+ bought in the past month.

why i recommend

three reasons this brand stands out

Sets in seconds, wicks into cracks

Thin CA is watery by design — it flows into hairline cracks and stabilizes them before you finish. The 2-3 second set time is why turners reach for it between lathe passes.

A kit that fights the #1 CA glue problem

Clogged nozzles kill most super glues. This kit includes precision micro-tips plus a pin-style anti-clog cap, so a $20 bottle does not die after three uses.

Small-batch freshness matters

CA glue degrades with age, and Starbond's whole pitch is small batches made to order since 1988 — 30-month shelf life if you store it under 40°F.

who it's for

honest match — no fluff

perfect for
  • Woodturners and woodworkers filling cracks and knots
  • Model builders and 3D-print hobbyists (PLA, ABS, PETG friendly)
  • Anyone tired of throwing away half-used super glue bottles
skip it if
  • You need gap-filling thick glue — that is their Medium/Black line
  • You want cheap hardware-store multi-packs
  • You have no ventilation — CA fumes are real, work near airflow

★★★★★

“As a follow up to my first post, many reviewers have cited problems using this runny CA adhesive on balsa wood & paper, a problem most liquid CA glues share. The trick to obtaining a great bond in these circumstances is to first apply a thin coating to the two edges to be bonded. Quickly WIPE THE EXCESS GLUE OFF THESE EDGES. Then let the glue sit for a while to harden. Finally, apply another VERY THIN coat of glue to the edges again, line everything up and press together. You'll get a darn near instant bond of INCREDIBLE strength. This product is VERY NOT suited for gluing things without flat edges/surfaces together, like the bathrobe hook problem another reviewer mentioned here. For this, you need a thick, non-runny adhesive. JB-WELD is a most impressive adhesive for applications like this. Apply a little to the coat hook screws and in the screw holes, cover the back of the hook with a thick coating of the stuff, then mount the hook to the door with the screws. The hook will never, ever come off the door again, promise! Even if the screw holes were stripped!”
