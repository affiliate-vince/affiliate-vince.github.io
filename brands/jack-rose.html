Jack & Rose

A cordless clipper-and-trimmer kit that's sold 9,300+ reviews' worth of home haircuts.

Jack & Rose is an Amazon-first home-goods brand whose biggest hit is this cordless clipper set — a full-size hair clipper plus a zero-gap T-blade trimmer for edges, sold with the guards, oil and brush you need to actually cut hair at home. The clipper runs 240 minutes per charge, uses carbon steel blades (sharper and faster than ceramic), and is quiet enough for kids. The listing holds 9,300+ ratings at 4.5 stars with 5,000+ bought in the past month. The brand also sells travel steamers and other home goods, so expect a grab-bag storefront — the clipper kit is the anchor.

why i recommend

three reasons this brand stands out

Clipper + zero-gap trimmer in one box

Full-size clipper for the bulk cut, small trimmer with a zero-gap T-blade for necklines, sideburns and beard edges — the two tools that cover a home haircut.

240 minutes per charge

Four hours of runtime means a full household of haircuts on one charge, and the cordless design works indoors or on the porch.

Quiet and kid-tolerant

Reviewers with wiggly children call out the low noise as the reason their kids stopped fighting haircuts — a small thing that makes home cuts possible.

who it's for

honest match — no fluff

perfect for
  • Families cutting hair at home to save barbershop trips
  • Beard and fade upkeep between pro cuts
  • People who travel and want one kit that does clipper + trimmer
skip it if
  • You are a barber needing industrial daily-duty clippers
  • You want a foil shaver for a clean-shaven finish — this is not that
  • You need it for pet grooming (different blade geometry)
