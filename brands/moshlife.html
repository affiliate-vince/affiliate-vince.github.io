MOSHLIFE

Protein bars founded by Maria Shriver and Patrick Schwarzenegger — 11g protein, 2g sugar, and brain-health ingredients.

MOSH is a brain-health food company started by Maria Shriver and her son Patrick Schwarzenegger. Their bars pair 11 grams of protein and 6 grams of fiber with ingredients aimed at cognition — 125 mg of Cognizin citicoline, plus lion's mane, ashwagandha, omega-3s and B12. They are vegan, keto-friendly, gluten-free and low-sugar (2g). The Amazon storefront is young but moving: the variety pack carries 2,000+ ratings at 4.1 stars.

why i recommend

three reasons this brand stands out

Brain-first formulation

Cognizin citicoline, lion's mane, ashwagandha, omega-3s — the ingredient list reads like a nootropic stack, except it is a snack you eat between meetings.

Clean label, real macros

11g protein, 6g fiber and only 2g sugar per bar, from plant sources. It is one of the few protein bars that is simultaneously vegan, keto-friendly and gluten-free.

Founders with a mission

Maria Shriver started MOSH after watching her father's Alzheimer's — the brand donates a portion of profits to women's Alzheimer's research. The mission is part of the packaging, literally.

who it's for

honest match — no fluff

perfect for
  • Afternoon snack when you want protein without a sugar crash
  • Keto or plant-based eaters tired of chalky bars
  • People who like their food brands to have a research story
skip it if
  • You want a candy-bar-style protein bar — these are mild
  • Nut allergies (check each flavor's ingredients)
  • You expect supplements in a bar to be a cognitive drug — it is food
