Hawaiian Shaved Ice

The S900A snow cone machine that has shaved 18,000+ households' worth of ice since 2011.

Hawaiian Shaved Ice's S900A is the snow cone machine that shows up in more summer birthday-party photos than any competitor. It has been on the market for well over a decade and holds 18,000+ ratings at 4.4 stars. It shaves bagged ice into fluffy snow with an adjustable stainless blade — fine powder for syrups, or coarser crunch for slush drinks — comes with reusable ice molds, a non-slip pad and a 1-year warranty, and stores about the size of a toaster.

why i recommend

three reasons this brand stands out

Real shaved ice, not crushed ice

The blade shaves ice into the soft, fluffy snow that soaks up syrup — different texture from the crunchy chunks a blender makes, and it matters for snow cones.

Texture you control

An adjustable blade lets you dial from fine powder (syrups, snow cones) to coarser shave (slushies, Italian sodas, granitas).

Built for party volume

Decades on the market and 18,000+ reviews mean the S900A has been through a lot of birthday parties. Bagged ice goes in, snow comes out, rinse and repeat.

who it's for

honest match — no fluff

perfect for
  • Parents hosting birthday parties or summer afternoons
  • Home bars making granitas, slushies or tiki drinks
  • Teachers and scout leaders running snow cone fundraisers
skip it if
  • You want a commercial countertop machine with continuous-duty motor
  • You only make one snow cone a year — it still needs storage space
  • You prefer pre-made sno-cone syrups (this makes the ice, not the syrup)
