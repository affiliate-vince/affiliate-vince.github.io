Mounting Dream

A tilt mount that's been bought over and over — 120,000+ ratings and still 4.7 stars.

Mounting Dream is one of the most-installed TV mount names on Amazon: the flagship tilt mount alone has passed 120,000 ratings at 4.7 stars, with 10,000+ bought in the past month. It fits most 37-90 inch TVs up to 120 lbs, sits about 1.5 inches off the wall, and tilts 8 degrees to cut glare. The line also covers full-motion, long-arm and low-profile mounts, several UL listed, plus a US-based support team that answers the phone.

why i recommend

three reasons this brand stands out

Proven at absurd scale

A 4.7-star average across more than 120,000 ratings is the kind of track record that is hard to fake. The flagship tilt mount holds up to 120 lbs and is one of Amazon's best-selling TV mounts year after year.

Tilt that fixes glare

An 8-degree forward tilt angles the screen toward your eyeline and away from window glare, without the cost or complexity of a full-motion arm. Sits just 1.5 inches off the wall when you want it flush.

Install is genuinely 3 steps

Mount the wall plate on 16-inch studs, hang the TV, lock it down. Hardware and instructions are included, and if something does not fit, the US-based team is reachable — reviewers mention getting replacement parts by email.

who it's for

honest match — no fluff

perfect for
  • 37-90 inch TVs on wood studs or concrete
  • People who want the TV close to the wall but need to tilt it toward the couch
  • First-time mounters who want a brand with an actual support team
skip it if
  • You need a swivel / pull-out arm — that is the full-motion line
  • Your TV is under 26 inches (too big a bracket)
  • Drywall-only walls with no stud to hit
