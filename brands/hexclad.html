HexClad

The hybrid pan Gordon Ramsay's name is on — 10,000+ ratings and a lifetime warranty.

HexClad made its name fusing stainless steel and nonstick into one surface: a laser-etched hexagonal grid over a ceramic nonstick layer, so you can sear like steel and still slide eggs out. This 12-inch pan with lid is the entry point — tri-ply with an aluminum core, induction-ready, oven and dishwasher safe, backed by a lifetime warranty when bought from HexClad directly. It carries 10,000+ ratings at 4.2 stars (about $183) and sits at the center of the Gordon Ramsay-endorsed lineup.

why i recommend

three reasons this brand stands out

Sear like steel, clean like nonstick

The hex grid keeps food off the flat nonstick surface — you get stainless-style browning on the raised steel, then the pan wipes clean. It is the compromise that made HexClad famous.

Induction-ready tri-ply

Aluminum core between steel layers heats evenly on any cooktop including induction, and the pan goes from stovetop to a 500°F oven without ceremony.

Lifetime warranty, in writing

HexClad backs pans sold through its own storefront with a lifetime warranty against manufacturer defects — unusually strong for cookware at this price.

who it's for

honest match — no fluff

perfect for
  • Home cooks who want stainless searing without stainless cleaning
  • Induction cooktop owners
  • People who keep killing nonstick pans with metal utensils and high heat
skip it if
  • You are a cast-iron purist
  • You want the cheapest nonstick that works for a year
  • You expect restaurant-grade stainless browning — hybrid is a middle ground, not a miracle
