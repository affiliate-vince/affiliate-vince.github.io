Dry Idea

The unscented antiperspirant for sensitive skin — 2x protection, 72-hour claims, zero fragrance.

Dry Idea is the legacy US deodorant brand that allergic and fragrance-sensitive women end up discovering after other sticks burn their underarms. The unscented solid (4-pack, 3.2 oz each) is hypoallergenic, dermatologist-tested and fragrance-free, with 2x antiperspirant protection and a 72-hour odor-control claim. 5,900+ ratings at 4.3 stars, and the wider line includes gels, dry spray and plant-based formulas.

why i recommend

three reasons this brand stands out

Made for reactive skin

No fragrance, hypoallergenic, dermatologist-tested — the whole brand identity is "antiperspirant for people whose skin hates antiperspirant."

Claims you can test

72-hour odor protection and 2x longer sweat protection versus ordinary antiperspirants are printed right on the stick — reviewers mostly confirm the all-day part.

No residue, no white marks

A fast-drying solid that reviewers say does not streak dark shirts or leave visible chalk — the unscented 4-pack is the value pick.

who it's for

honest match — no fluff

perfect for
  • Women with sensitive or eczema-prone underarms
  • People who react to fragrance in deodorants
  • Anyone who wants a no-nonsense, no-scent daily stick
skip it if
  • You want a scented deodorant
  • You need a men's-market product (this line is women's)
  • You prefer natural/aluminum-free formulas (this contains aluminum like any true antiperspirant)
