Glocusent

158,000+ ratings and still 4.7 — the neck book light that owns the category.

Glocusent's wrap-around neck reading light is one of the most-reviewed gadgets on Amazon: 158,000+ ratings at 4.7 stars, with 7,000+ bought in the past month. It wraps around your neck on flexible arms, shines a narrow 90-degree beam on your book instead of your partner's face, and runs up to 80 hours on a USB-C charge. Three color temperatures (3000K/4000K/6000K) with adjustable brightness cover everything from bedtime reading to knitting to late-night repair work — hands-free.

The 90-degree narrow beam is the design detail that made it famous: bright enough to read by, dim enough that your sleeping partner never notices.
