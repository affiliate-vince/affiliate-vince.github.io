Made By Dentists

A sonic toothbrush designed by dentists, with a 70-day battery — young listing, strong early reviews.

Made By Dentists is exactly what the name says: oral-care products designed by practicing dentists. Their current flagship is the PRO-X sonic toothbrush — up to 5x more plaque removal than a manual brush, three modes (sensitive, clean, whiten), a 2-minute timer with 30-second quadrant pulses, and a genuinely unusual 70-day battery life with a charging stand. It ships with three brush heads and a travel case, and carries a 2-year warranty. The listing is young (200+ ratings) but running at 4.6 stars with 600+ bought in the past month.

why i recommend

three reasons this brand stands out

Dentist-designed, dentist-positioned

The brand is built on clinician input — 5x plaque removal claims, gentle sonic vibration that protects enamel, and a 30-second quadrant timer that trains proper brushing.

70 days per charge

Most sonic brushes need weekly charging. The PRO-X runs up to 70 days on one charge and docks in a stand, so it survives travel and forgetful habits.

A 2-year warranty on a $50 brush

Two-year coverage against defects is generous at this price point — the kind of warranty that suggests the dentists actually stand behind it.
