Relaxium

The sleep supplement with 28,000+ honest ratings — including the people it didn't work for.

Relaxium Sleep is a non-habit-forming sleep supplement developed by Dr. Eric Ciliberti, a neurologist, with 5 mg melatonin, magnesium glycinate, passionflower, GABA, ashwagandha and chamomile plus its proprietary Valerest blend. It is one of the most-reviewed sleep aids on Amazon — 28,000+ ratings — and the reviews are genuinely split: a large camp says it is the first thing that worked, and a vocal minority says it did nothing or left them groggy. That mix is the real story here, and it is worth reading before you buy.

why i recommend

three reasons this brand stands out

Formulated by a neurologist

Dr. Eric Ciliberti designed the formula around his own patients who could not sleep — melatonin plus magnesium, passionflower, GABA and ashwagandha rather than a single-ingredient pill.

Aimed at staying asleep

The formula pairs a low 5 mg melatonin dose with calming minerals and botanicals, targeting the "fall asleep and stay asleep" problem instead of just knocking you out.

Refund guarantee behind it

Relaxium offers a 30-day money-back guarantee, which matters for a supplement with genuinely mixed reviews — you can try it against your own sleep and decide.

who it's for

honest match — no fluff

perfect for
  • Adults with occasional trouble falling or staying asleep
  • People who want a neurologist-formulated, non-habit-forming option
  • Buyers who read negative reviews and still want to try — the guarantee helps
skip it if
  • You need a stronger sedative effect — talk to a doctor
  • You are sensitive to melatonin (grogginess is the most common complaint)
  • You expect it to fix sleep apnea or serious insomnia on its own
