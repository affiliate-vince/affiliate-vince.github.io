Skin Grip

Waterproof CGM patches from a company whose tagline is 'as strong as you are.'

Skin Grip makes adhesive patches that hold continuous glucose monitors (and insulin pumps) on your skin through showers, swims and sweaty workouts. The flagship is a 20-pack of pre-cut, 4-way-stretch covers for Freestyle Libre 1 & 2 sensors and Lingo CGMs, made with medical-grade, hypoallergenic adhesive — no zinc oxide, no latex. 6,100+ ratings at 4.4 stars, with 1,000+ bought in the past month. They also make covers for Dexcom, Medtronic and Omnipod, plus universal patches and adhesive removers.

why i recommend

three reasons this brand stands out

Built for the sensor's whole life

The claim is 10+ days of hold through water and sweat — the entire wear window of a Libre sensor. Reviewers who lose sensors to workouts are the core audience, and they keep repurchasing.

Adhesive that respects skin

100% medical-grade adhesive, hypoallergenic, latex-free and without zinc oxide — the usual irritants. People with adhesive allergies are a big part of the 4.4-star fan base.

Pre-cut for your exact device

Patches are shaped for specific sensors and pumps, not generic tape — cut to cover Libre 1/2, Dexcom, Medtronic, Omnipod, Lingo and universal with a center hole for overpatch users.

who it's for

honest match — no fluff

perfect for
  • CGM users who lose sensors to sweat or snags
  • Swimmers, runners, gym-goers and hot-climate diabetics
  • Anyone whose skin reacts to standard medical tape
skip it if
  • You do not wear a CGM or pump (that is the whole product)
  • You need daily patch changes — these are long-wear
  • Your skin breaks out under any adhesive — test one patch first
