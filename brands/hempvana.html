Hempvana

5% lidocaine cream with hemp seed oil — the strongest OTC numbing strength, no pharmacy smell.

Hempvana is the As-Seen-on-TV pain relief brand whose Amazon lineup now leads with a max-strength cream: 5% lidocaine (the strongest OTC level, FDA-listed as a drug ingredient) blended with cold-pressed hemp seed oil. It targets localized pain — back, joints, knees, nerve discomfort — absorbs without grease and has no medicinal odor. The flagship cream sits at 4.3 stars across 6,400+ ratings; the family also includes a 2-pack soothing cream, a menthol/camphor extra-strength version and an Epsom-salt formula.

why i recommend

three reasons this brand stands out

Real numbing, not just cooling

5% lidocaine is the strongest concentration you can buy without a prescription. Applied to a sore spot it genuinely numbs — reviewers describe relief that outlasts menthol rubs by hours.

Hemp seed oil that does the heavy lifting

Cold-pressed hemp seed oil is deeply moisturizing, which matters when you are rubbing the same joint every night. The cream sinks in fast and leaves no grease on clothes or sheets.

No drugstore smell

The formula is odorless — no menthol cloud announcing you just applied pain cream. People use it at work and before bed without announcing anything.

who it's for

honest match — no fluff

perfect for
  • Localized joint, back and muscle pain
  • Nerve-type discomfort that responds to numbing
  • People who hate greasy, strong-smelling rubs
skip it if
  • You need whole-body relief (this is a targeted cream)
  • You are allergic to lidocaine or similar anesthetics
  • You expect a cure — it manages pain while on, it does not treat the cause
