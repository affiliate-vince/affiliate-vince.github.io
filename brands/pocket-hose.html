Pocket Hose

The expanding hose that claims 100+ patents and is currently moving 20,000+ units a month.

Pocket Hose has been the As-Seen-on-TV name in expanding hoses for years, and the current Ballistic line is the one shipping now: a 3/4-inch hose that expands to 3x its stored length, then drains and shrinks back small. The Ballistic sleeve uses liquid-crystal polymer fibers (the same family used in ballistic armor) over a 3-layer latex core, with a 360-degree swivel at the faucet to fight kinks. The 50-foot version shows 20,000+ bought in the past month across the line — and the reviews are honest about the trade-off: light and kink-resistant, but a smaller inner diameter than a traditional hose means lower flow.

why i recommend

three reasons this brand stands out

100 ft of reach that stores like 30

The core trick: hose expands 3x under water pressure and contracts when drained. A 50-footer coils into a small ring that hangs on a hook.

Light enough to actually drag around

Traditional rubber hoses get heavy fast. This one is built to be carried one-handed — the whole reason it dominates the "senior gardener" review demographic.

Faucet swivel that stops the twist

A 360-degree rotating connector at the spigot absorbs the twisting that makes regular hoses kink, and the Ballistic sleeve shrugs off UV and dragging.

who it's for

honest match — no fluff

perfect for
  • People who hate hauling and winding heavy hoses
  • Small-to-mid gardens, patios, car washing
  • Anyone who stores a hose in a bucket or on a hook
skip it if
  • You need full-pressure fire-hose flow (expanding hoses have smaller cores)
  • Constant commercial / daily heavy use — this is a home hose
  • You expect it to last a decade like a quality rubber hose — treat it as a 2-4 year tool and it will not disappoint
