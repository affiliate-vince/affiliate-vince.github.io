Prequel Skin

A 60% silicone scar stick that goes on like deodorant — from the brand dermatologists kept recommending.

Prequel Skin built its name on affordable, dermatologist-recommended skincare for sensitive and compromised skin — their Gleanser cleanser became a word-of-mouth phenomenon. The current Amazon flagship is Scar Solutions, a 60% silicone stick that works like silicone scar sheets but applies like a deodorant: it forms a watertight occlusive layer over scars, with centella asiatica to support healing skin. Fragrance-free, pH-stabilized, safe for dry and oily skin alike. 1,600+ ratings at 4.1 stars, $14.97.

why i recommend

three reasons this brand stands out

Silicone therapy without the sheets

Silicone is the dermatologist-standard approach to scar flattening — this delivers it as a stick you roll on, instead of adhesive sheets that peel off or show under clothes.

Derm-credible formulation

Fragrance-free and pH-stabilized with centella asiatica for compromised skin — the same sensibility as the Gleanser that made Prequel famous among dermatologists.

Discreet enough for the face

Dries to a thin, clear-ish film that works on face and body scars — surgical scars, acne marks, burns — and sits under sunscreen during the day.

who it's for

honest match — no fluff

perfect for
  • People with healing scars who hate silicone sheets
  • Post-surgery and post-acne scar care
  • Sensitive-skin users who react to fragrance-heavy scar creams
skip it if
  • Your scar is still an open wound (wait until it closes)
  • You want instant cosmetic results — silicone therapy takes weeks of consistent use
  • You expect a medical-grade prescription product
