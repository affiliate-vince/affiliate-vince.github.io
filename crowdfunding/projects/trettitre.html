Vince's honest take

Perfect for
  • People who want music hardware that looks like furniture — the wall-mounted format genuinely saves floor space and makes a statement.
  • Collectors who still own vinyl, CDs and cassettes and want one clean unit instead of three separate decks.
Skip it if
  • Audiophiles chasing reference-grade sound. This is an ambient lifestyle system, not a hifi statement — manage expectations on speaker quality.
  • Anyone who needs it by a specific date. This campaign funded in April; delivery timelines for late pledges depend on the current production run.

Risk & reality check

This is a funded, shipping campaign — but late pledges carry wait time. The original campaign funded in April at a 69,000%+ overfunding level, which is exactly the kind of demand that stretches production. Read the latest updates for current fulfillment status before pledging.

Sound quality is the open question. Wall-mounted retro units prioritize design; if your reference point is a dedicated turntable + speakers setup, audition expectations accordingly.

The good news: $2.07M raised with 4,972 backers and an active community (1,700+ comments) means this is a real product with real momentum — not a concept.

The bottom line

TRETTITRE is the least risky pick on this page — the campaign already funded and delivery is in motion. You are not betting on an idea; you are buying a design piece with a wait. If the look earns its wall space in your head, late-pledge pricing is the entry ticket.
